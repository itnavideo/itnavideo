1. YouTube Subtitle Generator

16:9 Landscape Fast-Path Engine 1080p Full HD

1.1 Identity & Specs

  • Dashboard Mode: mode: "youtubeSubtitleGenerator"
  • AI Registry Template: YOUTUBE_SUBTITLE_GENERATOR
  • Remotion Composition ID: AUTO-CAPTION-GENERATOR-LANDSCAPE
  • Resolution & Frame Rate: 1920 × 1080 (16:9 Landscape), 30 FPS
  • Duration Cap: Strictly up to 15 minutes (900 seconds).
  • Pricing Formula: calculateYouTubeSubtitleCreditUnits(durationSeconds) = 1 credit per 2 minutes (120s blocks). Max 8 credits for 15 mins.

1.2 Purpose & Creator Problem Solved

Long-form YouTube creators spend 3 to 6 hours typing and timing subtitles in Adobe Premiere or hiring freelancers on Fiverr. YouTube Subtitle Generator automates this completely in ~2 minutes with 5 high-retention creator styles.

1.3 Codebase Wiring

Layer File Path Responsibility
Frontend Dashboard app/dashboard/page.tsx Registers in videoTypeCards (long category), manages 15-min duration validation, and renders pricing badges.
Planner Registry services/ai/reelPlanner.ts Maps YOUTUBE_SUBTITLE_GENERATOR with skipPlanner: true and needsCaptionStylePicker: true.
Fast-Path API Route app/api/reels/jobs/route.ts Bypasses LLM planner, sends audio straight to Groq Whisper, and invokes renderMediaOnLambda().
Remotion Composition remotion/templates/AUTO_CAPTION_GENERATOR/ Renders video track and overlays word-synced subtitles in 16:9 bounds.
Client Preview components/preview/VideoTypePreviewRenderer.tsx Renders live 16:9 Remotion Player without charging credits.

1.4 The 5 Curated Creator Styles

  • Minimal Clean: Inter Sans, translucent dark pill background (Ali Abdaal / Veritasium).
  • Cinematic Docu: Montserrat Bold, off-white text with gold keywords (Vox / Johnny Harris).
  • Studio Podcast: Poppins Bold, matte black box for 100% legibility on busy video backdrops (Diary of a CEO).
  • Bold Creator: Montserrat ExtraBold, black outline with yellow pop animations (MrBeast / MKBHD).
  • Netflix Classic: Roboto Condensed, broadcast cinema yellow with drop shadow.

1.5 Common Errors & Technical Fixes

Error: Video Aspect Ratio Squishing / Black Bars

Root Cause: User uploads a vertical 9:16 or square 1:1 video into YouTube Subtitle Generator.
Fix: In remotion/templates/AUTO_CAPTION_GENERATOR/template.tsx, the <Video /> element uses object-fit: contain with a blurred mirrored background behind non-16:9 video so no content is stretched.

2. Auto Caption Generator (Reels / Shorts / TikTok)

9:16 Vertical Fast-Path Engine

2.1 Identity & Specs

  • Dashboard Mode: mode: "autoCaption"
  • AI Registry Template: AUTO_CAPTION_GENERATOR
  • Remotion Composition ID: AUTO-CAPTION-GENERATOR
  • Resolution & Frame Rate: 1080 × 1920 (9:16 Vertical), 30 FPS
  • Duration Cap: Up to 90 seconds (optimal for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok).
  • Pricing: 1 credit flat.

2.2 Purpose & Creator Problem Solved

Over 75% of social media users watch Reels and TikTok videos on mute. Without animated subtitles, viewers scroll away within the first 2 seconds. Auto Caption Generator adds kinetic, word-by-word bouncing captions with active word highlighting.

2.3 Codebase Wiring

  • remotion/components/MotionCaptionRenderer.tsx: Calculates the exact millisecond frame window for each word using useCurrentFrame() and applies pop scale transformations.
  • remotion/components/library/UniversalCaptionLayer.tsx: Handles safe-area padding to avoid Instagram and TikTok UI buttons (like, comment, share icons).

2.4 Common Errors & Technical Fixes

Error: Subtitle words overlapping screen buttons on TikTok/Instagram

Root Cause: Subtitle position is too close to the bottom edge.
Fix: Safe margins of bottom: 240px are enforced in UniversalCaptionLayer.tsx to keep text completely visible above user interface controls.

3. Compare Explainer Video

9:16 Vertical AI Scene Matcher

3.1 Identity & Specs

  • Dashboard Mode: mode: "compare"
  • AI Registry Template: comparisonImages
  • Remotion Composition ID: comparisonImages (mapped to CompareExplainer)
  • Resolution & Frame Rate: 1080 × 1920 (9:16 Vertical), 30 FPS
  • Duration Cap: 60 Seconds
  • Required User Inputs: 1 Audio voiceover + 2 Images (Left Item vs Right Item) + Titles (e.g. "iPhone 16" vs "S24 Ultra").

3.2 Purpose & Creator Problem Solved

Comparison videos ("Product A vs Product B", "Gym vs Home Workout", "Investing vs Real Estate") drive massive viral engagement on Reels. This template creates a split-screen layout with automated VS badges, sound effects, and voiceover synchronization.

3.3 Codebase Wiring

Layer File Path Responsibility
Composition Template remotion/templates/COMPARE_EXPLAINER/template.tsx Renders split layout: Left image, Right image, center VS emblem, progress bar.
Sound Effects Manifest remotion/templates/COMPARE_EXPLAINER/sfxManifest.ts Triggers punchy sound effects (whoosh, hit, gavel, buzzer) at key speech transitions.
Feature Flags services/ai/reelPlanner.ts needsImages: true, needsCompareFields: true.

3.4 Common Errors & Technical Fixes

Error: Images distorted or unevenly cropped in split screen

Root Cause: Left and right images have drastically different aspect ratios.
Fix: The template wraps each side in a responsive flexbox container with overflow: hidden and CSS object-fit: cover with subtle Ken Burns auto-pan so both sides look cinematic.

5. Whiteboard Video (Auto-Draw)

9:16 Vertical Educational Explainer

5.1 Identity & Specs

  • Dashboard Mode: mode: "whiteboardVideo"
  • Remotion Composition ID: WHITEBOARD-VIDEO
  • Inputs: Spoken audio or video explainer.

The system analyzes the speech transcript using NLP keyword extraction, creates bullet points and diagrams, and simulates hand-drawn SVG sketch reveals synchronized to speech pacing using Remotion's interpolate() stroke-dashoffset animations.

9:16 Vertical Content Repurposing

7.1 Identity & Specs

  • Dashboard Mode: mode: "longVideoClips"
  • Remotion Composition ID: LONG-VIDEO-CLIPS
  • Inputs: Long video file (podcast, interview, or lecture).

  • Remotion Composition ID: FACELESS-VIDEO

8.3 Common Errors & Technical Fixes

Error: Lambda Memory Crash on 15+ Min Faceless Videos

Root Cause: Pre-loading hundreds of high-resolution B-roll images and Canva backgrounds causes Chromium to exceed 2048 MB RAM.
Fix: Asset sequence rendering uses lazy-loaded image components with Remotion's Img component, and AWS Lambda function memory is allocated at 3008 MB.

10. AI Audio Cleaner

Audio Processor Studio Grade

10.1 Identity & Specs

  • Dashboard Mode: mode: "audioClean"
  • Inputs: Raw voiceover or podcast audio (up to 15 mins).
  • What it does:
    • De-Noising: Eliminates AC hum, computer fan noise, and background street rumble.
    • De-Reverb: Removes room echo from untreated rooms.
    • Dead Pause Cutting: Automatically detects silent gaps > 0.6s and trims them for snappy podcast pacing.
