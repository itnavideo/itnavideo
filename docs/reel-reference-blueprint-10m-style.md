# Deprecated: Old 10M+ Style Ultra-Advanced Reel Blueprint

Status: cancelled for future Video Explainer direction.

Use `docs/reel-reference-image-first-collage-rules.md` instead. The active direction is image-first collage reels with real photos/PNGs, typography, music, and SFX. Do not use this older icon/card-heavy blueprint as the main style source.

Reference notes for future reel planning, editing direction, sound design, color grading, and motion strategy.

## 1. The Hook (0:00 - 0:05) - The Aggressive Opener

Visual Strategy: Pure attention hijack. Hum sirf stock crowd nahi dikhayenge.

Advanced Editing Action:

The 3D Parallax Photo Cut: Crowd waali image ko Photoshop/After Effects mein cut karo. Foreground ke logo ko background se alag karo. Background ko halka sa blur karke upar move karo, aur logo ko camera ki taraf zoom karo (Z-axis movement).

The Text (Kinetic Typography): Ek bada bold font (jaise Montserrat Black ya Integral CF). Jab "10 LAKH APPLY" aaye, toh text ke andar ek Shatter or Glass Break effect ho.

Jab "1% SELECTION?" aaye, toh screen par ek bada Red Translucent "X" or Stamp overlay ho jo pure screen ko shake kar de (S Shake effect with chromatic aberration).

Sound Design:

0:00 - Ek heavy Cinematic Sub-Boom (jo earphones mein vibrate kare) + Distorted Crowd Roar (jo ek dum se cut ho jaye).

0:03 - Mechanical Camera Shutter + Metal Hit sound jab "1%" ka stamp lage.

## 2. The Reality Check (0:05 - 0:18) - The Data-Visualization

Visual Strategy: Vox-style data storytelling.

Advanced Editing Action:

The Paper-Rip Overlay: 0:05 par ek Paper Rip Transition ho jahan form-filling clip aayi hai. Us clip ke upar ek UI Overlay lagao (jaise computer screen ka reflection ya digital glitch borders).

The Infographic Scale-Down (0:12): Screen par 10 lakh dots ya human icons ka ek block dikhao jo pure screen par ho. Jaise hi "5,000 Vacancies" bole, screen ek dum se zoom-out ho aur wo bada block chota sa dot ban jaye, aur baki pura screen red ya black ho jaye. Yeh visual scale dikhayega ki 5,000 kitna chota number hai.

Sound Design:

0:05 - Vintage Projector hum / Film reel running sound.

0:12 - Ek continuous Deep Drone Sound jo scale-out hote hi ek Heavy Anvil Drop (Loha girne ki aawaz) ke saath ruk jaye.

## 3. The Pros & Cons (0:18 - 0:30) - The Illusion vs. Reality

Visual Strategy: Contrast matching.

Advanced Editing Action:

The Magazine Cut-Out Style (0:18): Govt office waali clip ke upar modern text mat lagao. "Job Security", "Pension", "Status" ko Newspaper clipping texture ke andar pop-up karwao, jaise kisi ne magazine se kaat ke screen par chipkaya ho (Stop-motion feel at 12fps).

The Heatmap Effect (0:24): Jaise hi competition ki baat ho, pure footage par ek Thermal / Heatmap filter (Red and Orange glow) daal do aur saturation badha do. Frame ke edges par Smoke/Fire element overlays use karo screen ko claustrophobic (tight) dikhane ke liye.

Sound Design:

0:18 - Har paper cut-out ke saath Paper Crumple / Tear SFX.

0:24 - Ek rising Heartbeat sound jo tez hoti jaye, background music ka high-end (treble) muffled ho jaye (Low-pass filter), jisse anxiety feel ho.

## 4. The Pivot to Private & Skills (0:30 - 0:40) - The Smooth Shift

Visual Strategy: Aesthetic minimalism. High-end creators yahan par mood change karte hain.

Advanced Editing Action:

The Match Cut / Seamless Pan (0:30): Piche ka dundhla crowd ek dum se swipe-left ho aur ek modern, clean corporate office ka glass window saamne aaye. Use a Directional Blur transition mixed with a Luma Fade (jo lighting ke hisab se blend ho).

The Glow-Text Tracking (0:36): Laptop chalaate hue bande ke haath ya laptop screen par Motion Tracking se text "Skills + Experience" ko attach kar do. Text ke piche ek halka sa Neon Drop Shadow Glow ho.

Sound Design:

0:30 - Ek bada Whoosh-to-Silence transition. Purana tense music band. Ek modern, aesthetic Lo-Fi Hip-Hop Beat with a crisp snare entry legi.

0:36 - Laptop typing ke saath digital "Beep-Boop" UI sound effects (modern synth plucks).

## 5. The Climax / Backup Plan (0:40 - 0:46) - The Realization

Visual Strategy: Split-screen rethink.

Advanced Editing Action:

The 3D Panel Split (0:40): Screen normal do hisso mein nahi batega. Left side ka frame tilted hokar piche chala jayega (3D Depth) aur Right side ka frame (Skills) bada hokar screen ke center mein aane lagega.

"Always Keep A Plan B" text ko screen ke center mein ek Bold Gradient Text (Yellow to Orange) mein rakho, aur uske piche ek Glitch RGB Split effect lagao jo har 2-2 frames baad twitch kare.

Sound Design:

0:40 - Heavy Riser SFX (jo sound dheere dheere build hoti hai) aur ek Slam / Cinematic Impact jab Plan B text freeze ho.

## 6. The Call to Action (0:46 - 0:50) - The Loop Master

Visual Strategy: Instagram Algorithm Loop.

Advanced Editing Action:

The Dynamic UI Mockup: Screen par Instagram ka comment box ka ek clean 3D render popup karega. Text "What's Your Plan B?" ke niche ek Typing Indicator (three dots blinking) animation chalao, jo viewer ko type karne ke liye psychologically trigger kare.

The Seamless Loop Setup: Video ke last frame ka background color aur position bilkul exact pehle frame (0:00) jaisa match kar do, taaki jab reel khatam ho, toh viewer ko pata hi na chale ki video kab repeat ho gayi.

Sound Design:

0:46 - Music bilkul fade-out ho jaye, sirf ek clean Notification Bell / Pop sound aaye. Last word ke baad sound bilkul cut, silent ambient noise jo seamlessly loop ke pehle frame ke sub-boom se connect ho jaye.

## Pro-Level Editing Pack

Color Grading: Pura video flat mat rakhna. Govt sector/tension waale part ko De-saturated Blues/Greys do, aur Private/Skills waale part ko Vibrant Teal & Orange tone do.

Framing (Aspect Ratio): Text hamesha 4:5 ke safe zone mein rakhna taaki feed par bhi text na kate.

Frame Rate Jitter: Newspaper/Magazine cutouts ko 12 FPS par animate karna, aur baki video ko smooth 60 FPS par rakhna. Yeh visual contrast 10M+ videos ki nishaani hai.
