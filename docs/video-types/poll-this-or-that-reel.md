Engagement polls and comparison reels. "iPhone vs Samsung?", "Tea or Coffee?". Designed to drive comments and shares.

| Input | Type | Required |
|-------|------|----------|
| Audio/Video | Audio/Video | Yes |

## Key Rules
- Two-option visual layout (A vs B or poll style)
- Engagement-optimized design (drives comments)
- Options extracted from transcript
- Clean, shareable, social-native feel
- No background music by default
